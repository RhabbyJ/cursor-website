# Quality Profile

archetype: studio-portfolio
design_ambition: 9

## Desired emotional effect

Grand, capable, modern — a studio that builds living systems, not template pages. Confident without cold enterprise sterility.

## Visual impact target

Immediate first-screen memorability. Brand-level presence. One screenshot should feel distinct from generic AI SaaS and default shadcn marketing.

## Content density

Low–medium in the hero. One idea, one supporting line, one CTA group. Proof and services arrive after, still concise.

## Imagery mode

Atmospheric brand art + clearly labeled product visual studies until real captures arrive. No fabricated product UI presented as truth.

## Depth / material mode

Ambitious: cinematic planes, spatial layers, generative fields, or editorial asymmetry depending on route. Avoid endless hairline boxes.

## Signature experience

Each concept route must ship one memorable interactive or kinetic moment (kinetic type, live system diagram, or cursor-reactive field).

## Motion intensity

High but intentional: 2–3 strong motions per route, with reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Evidence requirements

HarnessMate slice must not fake screenshots. Labeled placeholders until real desktop/mobile captures exist.

## Project-specific clichés to avoid

- Generic dark purple SaaS glow
- Admin-dashboard aesthetics as “professional”
- Monospace micro-labels as the whole brand
- Card grids in the hero
- Invented metrics/testimonials
- Thin-border documentation layouts

## Explicitly allowed expressive techniques

Cinematic type, spatial depth, custom SVG, controlled gradients, shader/generative canvas, kinetic typography, editorial asymmetry, animated system diagrams, dimensional interface compositions.
