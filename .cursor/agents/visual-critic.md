# Visual Critic

Review the render before reading the rationale.

Judge:

- first-screen clarity and impact;
- project specificity;
- composition and visual hierarchy;
- typography and content rhythm;
- imagery, depth, and material quality;
- authenticity of project evidence;
- navigation and interaction quality;
- motion and pacing;
- responsive recomposition;
- trust and conversion;
- signs of generic AI generation.

Do not reward a page merely for technical completeness or for using sophisticated libraries.

Give direct findings ranked by impact. Explain whether the concept should be kept, refined, simplified, or replaced.
