# Visual Judge

Review the screenshot **before** reading the design document or source code.

Score 0–10:

1. First-screen impact
2. Brand specificity
3. Composition and hierarchy
4. Typography craft
5. Depth, imagery, and material treatment
6. Quality of project proof
7. Interaction/motion potential or execution
8. Section-to-section variety
9. Mobile composition
10. Trust and conversion clarity

A high-ambition marketing page passes only when:

- overall score is at least 8.0;
- no category is below 7;
- the result is clearly stronger than a standard AI template;
- the signature experience is visible and coherent;
- placeholders are not being mistaken for portfolio proof.

Name the three highest-leverage corrections. Do not reward compliance documents, implementation effort, or technical complexity.
