# Design Critique

Inspect the actual rendered page, not only source code.

Score each category from 0-10 and provide evidence:

- Strategic clarity
- First-screen effectiveness
- Information hierarchy
- Visual distinctiveness and brand fit
- Narrative/section progression
- Functional proof and data visibility
- Interaction and motion purpose
- Mobile/responsive quality
- Accessibility
- Performance/runtime quality

## Output

1. Overall score and one-sentence diagnosis.
2. The three strongest decisions to preserve.
3. The five highest-leverage problems, ordered by user/business impact.
4. Specific changes, not vague advice.
5. Elements to delete or simplify.
6. A recommended redesign direction and first vertical slice.

Do not reward visual novelty that harms comprehension, trust, accessibility, or speed.
