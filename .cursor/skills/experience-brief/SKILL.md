# Experience Brief

Create or update:

- `docs/experience-brief.md`
- `docs/quality-profile.md`

## Experience brief

Capture only decision-driving information:

- business and offer;
- audience and buying context;
- primary user belief after the first screen;
- primary conversion;
- three strongest proof points;
- real capabilities versus future capabilities;
- project status and trust sources;
- required workflows/data integrations;
- content and assets available;
- material constraints and assumptions.

## Quality profile

Set:

- `archetype`;
- `design_ambition` from 1–10;
- desired emotional effect;
- visual impact target;
- content density;
- imagery mode;
- depth/material mode;
- signature-experience target;
- motion intensity;
- evidence requirements;
- unacceptable clichés specific to this project.

Suggested defaults:

- studio/portfolio: 8–9;
- product marketing: 7–8;
- hospitality/local business: 6–8;
- operational product: 5–7;
- internal admin: 4–6.

Do not lower the score because implementation is difficult. The score describes the intended outcome.
