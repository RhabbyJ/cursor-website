# Visual Exploration

Use `/art-director` and build exactly three routes under a temporary route group such as:

```text
/visual-routes/a
/visual-routes/b
/visual-routes/c
```

Each route must include:

- the real proposed hero copy;
- navigation treatment;
- one proof/case-study slice;
- a signature visual or interaction prototype;
- desktop and mobile composition;
- a short source/candidate manifest.

## Material difference requirement

Routes must differ in at least five of these:

- layout and focal point;
- typography character;
- light/dark/material treatment;
- imagery or generative-art strategy;
- spatial depth;
- navigation behavior;
- signature interaction;
- case-study presentation;
- motion character.

Changing color, border radius, or card style is not a new route.

## Tool use

Use `docs/tool-preflight.md` and `component-scout`. High-ambition routes should incorporate or meaningfully adapt selected references/components when they improve the concept.

## Output

Capture desktop and mobile screenshots to `docs/visual-routes/`. Fill `docs/visual-routes.md` with the differences, assets, candidate components, dependencies, and known risks.

Do not continue to the full page until one route is selected.
