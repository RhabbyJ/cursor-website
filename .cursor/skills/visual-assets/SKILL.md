# Visual Assets

## Asset hierarchy

Prefer the source that best serves the concept:

1. real product, client, location, people, or process imagery;
2. commissioned or project-specific illustration and brand art;
3. carefully generated decorative imagery;
4. custom SVG, CSS, canvas, or 3D compositions;
5. generic stock imagery only when appropriate and honestly representative.

Do not fabricate portfolio screenshots, customer logos, testimonials, team members, metrics, or product functionality.

## Product work

Use actual screenshots or recordings whenever available. If assets are missing, use clearly labeled placeholders and request or generate only non-evidentiary supporting art.

## Generated art

Cursor image generation may create:

- abstract brand worlds;
- textures;
- editorial illustrations;
- hero imagery;
- diagrams that do not imply false facts;
- atmospheric backgrounds.

Record the prompt and intended use. Optimize and crop the resulting asset for the real layout.

## Custom visuals

Use custom SVG, canvas, WebGL, 3D, or CSS when the concept benefits from interaction, precise scaling, data-driven behavior, or a signature visual that cannot be achieved with static imagery.

Always provide fallbacks and account for performance and reduced motion.
