# Visual Direction

Use `/creative-director` where available and save the decision to `docs/visual-direction.md`.

## Required output

- Desired user belief and emotional tone
- Three genuinely different candidate directions
- Decision matrix and selected direction
- Typography roles and scale character
- Color roles, contrast strategy, and surface hierarchy
- Layout grammar, container behavior, density, and section rhythm
- Image/illustration/product-demo approach
- Signature motif or interaction tied to the product
- Motion character and reduced-motion equivalent
- Mobile adaptation, not just stacking
- Explicit anti-patterns

A visual direction must be implementable with the project's content and assets. Do not choose a cinematic image-led direction when no credible imagery exists, or a dense data direction when the product has no real data to show.
