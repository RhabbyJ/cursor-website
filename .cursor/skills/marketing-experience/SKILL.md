# Marketing Experience

## First-screen contract

The initial viewport should make clear:

- who this is for;
- what valuable outcome is offered;
- why this option is credible or different;
- what the visitor can do now;
- a visual or interactive piece of proof.

## Narrative

Choose sections from user questions, not a fixed template. Typical questions include:

- Is this for me?
- What can it do or change?
- How does it work operationally?
- Can I trust it?
- What does it cost or require?
- What should I do next?

Show the system behind the website. For data-powered sites, demonstrate the live menu, events, listings, availability, calculator, workflow, or admin update path rather than presenting only brand copy.

Use truthful proof. When quantitative proof is unavailable, use process transparency, live examples, credentials, documented methodology, or a clearly labeled demonstration.
