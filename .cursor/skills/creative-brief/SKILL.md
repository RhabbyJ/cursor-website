# Creative Brief

Capture only decisions that change the design:

- product, offer, or experience;
- audience and arrival context;
- primary action;
- core promise;
- strongest available proof;
- content hierarchy;
- desired emotional effect;
- visual ambition and motion intensity;
- real assets and references;
- brand traits and anti-traits;
- functional requirements;
- trust, accessibility, and performance constraints.

Do not fill gaps with familiar agency or SaaS language. Mark assumptions clearly.

The brief must not prescribe dark mode, gradients, card grids, bento layouts, glass, monospace, or minimalism unless the project evidence supports them.

End with a one-sentence creative challenge, such as:

> Make a complex engineering product feel immediately trustworthy without making it feel bureaucratic.

The wording must be unique to the project.
