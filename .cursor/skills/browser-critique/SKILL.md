# Browser Critique

Inspect the real site in Cursor Browser or another supported browser tool.

## Capture

Capture the important states at target sizes:

- first screen;
- navigation open and scrolled states;
- representative content sections;
- signature interaction;
- forms or primary workflow;
- mobile and desktop.

## Screenshot-first review

The visual critic should inspect screenshots before reading the design rationale or source code.

Evaluate:

- immediate clarity;
- emotional and brand fit;
- composition and hierarchy;
- typography;
- imagery and depth;
- originality versus familiar AI patterns;
- section variety;
- component consistency;
- project proof;
- motion quality;
- responsive recomposition;
- trust and conversion.

## Interaction review

Operate the navigation, primary CTA, forms, galleries, accordions, menus, and signature effects. Check console and network output where available.

## Findings

Rank findings by impact:

- concept-level;
- composition-level;
- content-level;
- interaction-level;
- polish-level.

Prefer subtraction or recomposition over adding decorative effects to a weak layout.
